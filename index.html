RBI Percentage

RBI% (RBI − HR) ÷ ROB — the share of the runners on base that a hitter drove home. ROB is every runner standing on base during his plate appearances, counted one at a time: a man on first is one, bases loaded is three. Home runs come off the top because that run is the hitter driving himself in.
CL% (2-out RBI − 2-out HR) ÷ 2-out ROB — the same rate with two outs, when nobody behind him gets another swing that inning. Click any heading to sort.

RBI Percentage

RBI% RBI ÷ RISP — the share of his chances a hitter cashed. RISP is every runner in scoring position while he batted, counted one at a time: second and third on the bases is two, a man on first is none. Every RBI counts in the top line, home runs included.
CL% Σ(RBI × LI) ÷ Σ(RISP × LI) — the same ratio with every plate appearance multiplied by its leverage index, MLB’s measure of how much that moment swings the game. A bases-loaded ninth counts several times a blowout. LI is his average leverage. Diff is how many places a hitter moves from the RBI leaderboard to the RBI% leaderboard — green means the rate likes him more than the raw count does.

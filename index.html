RBI% RBI ÷ RISP — the share of his chances a hitter cashed. RISP is every runner in scoring position while he batted, counted one at a time: second and third on the bases is two, a man on first is none. A walk, intentional walk or hit-by-pitch is not a chance at all — he never got a pitch to swing at — so those trips leave both halves of the fraction, a forced-in run going with the runners it was forced from. Every other RBI counts in the top line, home runs included. Tick Count walks as chances to put them back. CL% Σ(RBI × LI) ÷ Σ(RISP × LI) is the same ratio with each plate appearance multiplied by its leverage index, so the big moments count more.

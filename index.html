RBI Percentage

RBI% RBI ÷ RISP — the share of his chances a hitter cashed. RISP is every runner in scoring position while he batted, counted one at a time: second and third on the bases is two, a man on first is none. Every RBI counts in the top line, home runs included. CL% Σ(RBI × LI) ÷ Σ(RISP × LI) is the same ratio with each plate appearance multiplied by its leverage index, so the big moments count more.
